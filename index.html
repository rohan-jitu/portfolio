FAQs
Can you do SEO for custom-coded websites or other CMS platforms?

Yes. I can work with Shopify, WordPress, custom-coded websites, and other CMS platforms. The SEO strategy is tailored to your website's technology, structure, industry, and business goals.

How long does SEO take to show results?

SEO is a long-term strategy, and results vary depending on competition, website authority, technical health, content quality, and target keywords. Some improvements can appear within a few weeks, while competitive keywords may take longer.

Can you guarantee first-page or #1 Google rankings?

No ethical SEO professional can guarantee a specific Google ranking. My focus is on implementing proven SEO strategies, improving search visibility, and building sustainable organic growth based on measurable data.

What exactly will you do for my website?

I analyze your website, technical SEO, keywords, competitors, content, site structure, internal linking, and search performance. Based on the findings, I create and implement a prioritized SEO strategy focused on your biggest growth opportunities.

Can you increase my website traffic and sales?

Yes. The goal of SEO is not simply to increase traffic but to attract relevant users who are more likely to become customers. I focus on high-intent keywords, search visibility, content, technical improvements, and conversion-focused organic growth.

How will I know if SEO is working?

I track important SEO and business metrics including rankings, impressions, clicks, organic traffic, CTR, landing-page performance, and conversions. You'll receive clear reporting so you can see how the campaign is progressing.

Can you show me your SEO results or case studies?

Yes. I can share relevant SEO results, project examples, and case studies where available. Results can vary by industry, competition, website condition, and campaign duration.

How long do I need to continue SEO?

SEO works best as an ongoing growth strategy. Once the foundation is established, continued optimization, content development, technical improvements, competitor monitoring, and performance analysis help maintain and expand organic visibility.
