FAQ

Questions I get before we start

Can you do SEO for custom-coded websites or other CMS platforms?

Yes. I work with Shopify, WordPress, custom-coded websites and other CMS platforms. The strategy is tailored to your website's technology, structure, industry and business goals.

How long does SEO take to show results?

SEO is a long-term strategy, and results vary with competition, website authority, technical health, content quality and target keywords. Some improvements appear within a few weeks, while competitive keywords take longer.

Can you guarantee first-page or #1 Google rankings?

No ethical SEO professional can guarantee a specific Google ranking. My focus is implementing proven SEO strategies, improving search visibility and building sustainable organic growth based on measurable data.

What exactly will you do for my website?

I analyze your website, technical SEO, keywords, competitors, content, site structure, internal linking and search performance. From those findings I create and implement a prioritized strategy focused on your biggest growth opportunities.

Can you increase my website traffic and sales?

The goal is not simply more traffic but relevant users who are likely to become customers. I focus on high-intent keywords, search visibility, content, technical improvements and conversion-focused organic growth.

How will I know if SEO is working?

I track rankings, impressions, clicks, organic traffic, CTR, landing-page performance and conversions. You receive clear reporting so you can see exactly how the campaign is progressing.

Can you show me your SEO results or case studies?

Yes. I can share relevant SEO results, project examples and case studies where available. Results vary by industry, competition, website condition and campaign duration.

How long do I need to continue SEO?

SEO works best as an ongoing growth strategy. Once the foundation is set, continued optimization, content development, technical improvements, competitor monitoring and performance analysis maintain and expand organic visibility.
